rostdav-mfa is the phone half of the sign-in to your own Rostdav server.

When you sign in to Rostdav in a browser, your phone gets a request. It shows the service, the device and the rough place of the sign-in. You pick the number the browser shows, confirm with your fingerprint or face, and the browser signs in. If the number is wrong, or you tap Deny, the sign-in is refused.

Why it is safe
• The approval key is created in the phone's hardware-backed keystore and never leaves it. It is only used after your fingerprint or face.
• Every request and every answer is signed. The server checks the signature, the number and the time.
• No account, no cloud service, no tracking. The app talks only to your Rostdav server.
• The default build needs no Google services at all. It finds new requests by checking in while the app is open.
• The approval screen blocks screenshots and the recents preview.
• Lost the phone? Remove it from your Rostdav account; it can never approve a sign-in again.

What you get
• Number matching, so a request you did not start cannot be approved by accident.
• An overview of waiting requests, with a count on the Requests tab.
• Your phones and the latest sign-in events, and a way to remove a phone.
• Dark and light themes, English and German.

What you need
• A Rostdav server with phone sign-in switched on. You connect by scanning the QR code in Rostdav's web settings, or by typing the address and the one-time token.

Coming later
• An authenticator vault for one-time codes, stored encrypted on the phone. It is planned and not part of this version.
